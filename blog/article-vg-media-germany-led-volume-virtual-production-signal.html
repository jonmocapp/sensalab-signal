A marketing agency building its own LED volume signals in-house virtual production

Edition 21·02 Aug 2026·3 min read

A production agency near Stuttgart has built a 100 square metre LED volume with broadcast-grade processing, not a render farm. The signal is that virtual production is moving in-house at agency scale, owned rather than rented.

VG Media Studio, a marketing and production agency in Laichingen near Stuttgart, has opened a virtual production facility built around an LED volume of more than 100 square metres of LG panels, according to Installation International. Two 4K Brompton Tessera SX40 processors drive the main wall and a Tessera S8 drives the ceiling, with each SX40 paired to two Tessera XD 10G data boxes, while Mo-Sys handles camera tracking. The stage runs at up to 60 frames per second, with 120fps possible after further configuration, and leaves about 250 square metres of floor in front of the wall, alongside a separate 70 square metre room for conventional film and photography. The agency says the project grew out of talks with LG about becoming an official European reference studio.

The real signal is ownership, not kit

The specification is impressive, but the hardware is not the signal. The signal is who bought it. This is a marketing and production agency standing up a permanent LED volume for its own work, rather than renting a stage per shoot or sending virtual production out to a specialist house. That turns a capability most agencies treat as an outsourced line item into fixed infrastructure the agency owns, schedules and resells. A reference-studio arrangement with a panel maker also means the room is built to be shown, not only shot in, which raises the bar on how it is run.

What a producer can do now

The in-house move changes the maths on a campaign. A standing volume with a 250 square metre floor and a 70 square metre side room can carry a content shoot in the morning and a product capture in the afternoon, which only pays off at volume. A producer weighing an owned stage against per-day rental should look at utilisation across a full year, not a single brief, and specify the processing and tracking for the most demanding job the room will ever take, since 120 frames per second and accurate camera tracking are decided at install, not recovered later in the edit.

Why it belongs in an experiential toolkit

An LED volume is not only a film tool. The same calibrated wall, real-time backgrounds and camera tracking that serve a commercial shoot also drive live stage content, broadcast sets and in-person brand stages. For an agency, treating the volume as shared infrastructure rather than a film set widens what it can sell from the same room. That is the logic behind a reusable shows kit: one calibrated real-time stage, specified once and pointed at many briefs, rather than a bespoke build commissioned each time a client asks for virtual production.

Treat a permanent LED volume as a utility the agency rents back to itself, and spec the processing and tracking for the most demanding job the room will ever take, not the opening brief.

Why it matters

When a marketing agency, not a studio or a rental house, commissions its own LED volume, virtual production stops being a line item it outsources and becomes a standing capability it owns, schedules and resells.
