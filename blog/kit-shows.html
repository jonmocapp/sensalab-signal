10 ideas for shows

Ten ways to make stage visuals the main act, not the backdrop. Each pairs a concept with the tech behind it.

01

A wraparound world

A real time 3D world on the LED wall that shifts with the set. LED wall plus real time.

02

A reactive stage

Visuals that respond to music, lighting or performer position in real time. Real time plus tracking.

03

A volumetric performer

Capture or extend a performer into the digital world, live. Volumetric plus real time.

04

A generative act

A segment where the visuals are generated live and never repeat. Generative.

05

An audience lit finale

The crowd's phones or wearables become part of the show's lighting. Networked lighting.

06

Camera aware content

Visuals composed for the broadcast and stream camera as much as the room. Virtual production.

07

Scene teleports

Instant world changes between songs or segments on the LED volume. Real time 3D.

08

An AR extension

An app or WebAR layer that adds a dimension only phones can see. AR.

09

Data driven visuals

Set visuals that pull from live inputs, tour data or socials, to feel current. Real time data.

10

A pre show world

An immersive lobby world that primes the audience before doors. Projection or LED.
