Privacy

Last updated 2 August 2026.

The Signal is published by SensaLab, a Los Angeles experiential creative studio. This note explains, in plain terms, what we collect and why.

What we collect

If you subscribe to the newsletter or request a free kit, we collect the email address you give us, and which kit you asked for. That is it. We do not ask for anything else.

Why we collect it

To send you The Signal every two weeks, to deliver the kit you requested, and, if you write to us, to reply. We do not sell or rent your email, and we do not share it beyond the tools we use to send email.

Your choices

Every email includes a one click unsubscribe. You can also write to hello@sensalab.io at any time to see what we hold or to have it deleted.

Analytics

We may use privacy friendly, aggregate analytics to understand what is read. It does not identify you personally.

Contact

Questions about this note or your data: hello@sensalab.io. SensaLab, Los Angeles, California, USA.
