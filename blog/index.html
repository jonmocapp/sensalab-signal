About SensaLab

SensaLab is a Los Angeles experiential creative studio.

We are the white label real time 3D and immersive layer that agencies and brands use to build interactive activations under their own name. We work in real time 3D, virtual production and LED volumes, spatial and augmented experiences, immersive installations and live brand activations, and we ship them white label so the work goes out under your brand, not ours.

Based in Los Angeles, California. See more at sensalab.io or write to hello@sensalab.io.
