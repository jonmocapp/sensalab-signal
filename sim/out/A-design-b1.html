The World Cup's best experiences happened outside the stadiums

Spain took the trophy at MetLife on July 19. Producers should study everything around it: fan festivals at two-million scale, a drone scoreboard over Seattle, and ticketed dome watch parties.

01 Field Notes

The activations that worked gave fans a job to do

Michelob ULTRA ran a two-week Pitchside Club on Santa Monica Pier with a 2,500-drone show; adidas built free Home of Soccer hubs across five cities with 3v3 tournaments. The pattern across Event Marketer's roundup: watching was table stakes. The activations people queued for handed them a ball, a game, a role.

In the Lab

The scoreboard in the sky was really a data pipeline

Visit Seattle flew a 400-drone scoreboard beside the Space Needle after each hosted match: 12-minute shows rendering the score, flags and city imagery, billed as a first. The steal: any same-day feed — scores, votes, sales — can become sky-scale scenography.

03 Craft

The watch party is now a venue-grade product

Fox Sports and FIFA put roughly 40 matches, the final included, inside Cosm's 87-foot 12K domes in LA, Dallas and Atlanta — with stadium cameras framed for the dome, not TV. The craft lesson: capture designed for the canvas beats repurposed broadcast.
