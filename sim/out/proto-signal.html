This week's signal

The best of the World Cup
happened outside the stadiums.

Spain lifted the trophy at MetLife on July 19. But the playbook it left for brands and producers was written on the sidewalks, in the sky, and inside a dome. Three signals worth stealing.

01
The activations people remembered
didn't ask to be watched.

Michelob ULTRA ran a two-week Pitchside Club with a 2,500-drone show; adidas built free Home of Soccer hubs with 3v3 pitches across five cities. Watching was table stakes. The ones people queued for handed them a ball, a game, a role.

Why it mattersParticipation outlasts impressions.

02
A scoreboard in the sky is just a
data pipeline with an audience.

Visit Seattle flew a 400-drone scoreboard beside the Space Needle after every hosted match — 12-minute shows rendering the score, the flags, the city. Billed as a first.

Why it mattersAny same-day feed can become sky-scale scenography.

03
The watch party grew up. It's a
venue-grade product now.

Fox Sports and FIFA put roughly 40 matches, the final included, inside Cosm's 87-foot 12K domes in LA, Dallas and Atlanta — with cameras framed for the dome, not the TV.

Why it mattersCapture built for the canvas beats repurposed broadcast.
